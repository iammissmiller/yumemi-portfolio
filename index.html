旅

My story

Five places, one little kitten. Keep scrolling to walk with me.

  1. stop 1 / 5

    The old city gate

    Hi, I'm Yumemi. I'm a calico kitten, black, orange and white, and I've been a baby for as long as anyone can remember.

  2. stop 2 / 5

    The midnight noodle stall

    I follow my nose from town to town. Every place has a bowl of something warm, and I try every single one.

  3. stop 3 / 5

    The kite playground

    Little ones are my favourite people. They play like today is all there is, and they're right.

  4. stop 4 / 5

    The sakura shrine

    The petals only stay a few days. So I stop, watch, and squeeze into the sticker booth to keep one moment, every time.

  5. stop 5 / 5

    The rooftop teahouse

    I never grow up, so I never have to hurry. Sit with me a while. The tea is still warm.

通行手形Travel permit

Name
Yumemi ゆめみ
What I am
A baby calico kitten, black, orange and white, who never grows up. Usually found in a lilac hoodie, big headphones and platform sneakers, with a paper lantern on a stick.
What I do
I wander across East Asia with my lantern, eat every local dish I find and paint the road as the seasons change. Wherever I stop, I leave a small letter for children about loving childhood and living in the moment.
My story
Long ago, on the last night of a lantern festival, I made a wish on the only lantern still glowing: to stay little until I had tasted every bowl and met every child. The lantern said yes. So I carry it with me, and I keep walking.
Motto
Stay little, and eat it while it's warm ♡
